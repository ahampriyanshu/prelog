# Religion and philosophy update — 31 October 2026

The catalogue contains 211 entries, including 75 additions. Identifiable names
replace display labels such as “sacred worlds”, “traditions” and “flowerings”.
Source titles retain their published wording.

The additions cover Ahmadiyya and its Qadian and Lahore branches; the original
Nirankari Sikh movement and the separate Sant Nirankari Mission; Namdhari,
Ravidassia, Radhasoami and Beas; specific Sufi, Ismaili, Jain and Hindu branches;
Latter-day Saints, Community of Christ, Scientology, Adventists, Jehovah’s
Witnesses, Anabaptist churches and named Eastern churches; Bön, East Asian
Buddhist schools, Kegon, Cao Đài, Hòa Hảo, Japanese new religions and named
African diaspora religions.

Each addition retains its source, aliases, relationship, date qualification and
individual geographic settings. Supporting location references appear in the
sidebar. A founding or institutional date is distinguished from a much older
history. Display interpolation endpoints are excluded from the additions’
historical-stage lists. All 75 additions have a continuing modern setting,
including Huayan's Japanese reception through Kegon and Mustaʿli continuity
through Tayyibi communities; those are not additive population estimates.

The modern population layer covers 201 source countries and territories,
representing 99.98% of the world's population in Pew's study. It uses dated
2010/2020 observations, explicit missing-data hatching, minority shares and
unaffiliated identity. See `data/religion/README.md` for provenance and joins.

This is a broader sourced catalogue, not an assertion that every religion,
local lineage, historical community or denomination has now been catalogued.
Historical setting polygons remain illustrative, not surveyed religious borders.

Validation covers naming, aliases, distinct identities, source references,
ordered dates, current presence, country joins, minority shares, observation
dates, and preservation of the approved India geometry. Browser checks include
country details, catalogue search, forward/reverse scroll, pre-founding dates
and mobile layouts at 390 × 844.

## Unified area map · 31 October 2026

Schools & ideas is now the default throughout the Religion & Philosophy timeline.
The same geographic renderer is used before and after 2010. Population estimates
remain an optional view for the two measured years; they are loaded only when that
view is requested. Their country dates and source limitations are unchanged.

Names are SVG text inside their mapped areas, with wrapping, measured text widths,
coast/land checks and collision avoidance. Labels stay attached to geography during
camera transitions. No label cards, detached names or +N badges are rendered.
Smaller areas retain their interactive geometry, keyboard access and compact name
tooltips. Zooming can reveal labels that did not fit. A school can be named in up to three separate settings, so an overseas label does not suppress its name at home. Selecting a place preserves a readable regional zoom and positions it above the phone detail sheet. Country population overlays
also carry an affiliation name where it fits.

Each family uses one colour and a union clip, avoiding repeated alpha accumulation
from overlapping branches of the same family. All mapped settings remain present
when a school is selected. Ancestors and descendants are emphasised; the dossier
separates lineage, direct branches and other relationships. No new connections,
religious borders, historical extents or population estimates are inferred.

The shared adaptive time controls end at 2020 followed by Today, include recorded
stages, and support reversible scroll/keyboard/button navigation. Area layers
cross-fade for 700 ms, retaining only the current and previous date. Reduced-motion
preferences disable the transition. `map-labels.js` and `thought-map.js` contain
the geometry-aware label placement and timeline logic respectively.
